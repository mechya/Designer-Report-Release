# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 22, 2026*

Designer Report ("the app") is a desktop application made by Gurung Bhupesh. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your files stay on your computer

Reports, data sources, fonts, images and settings are stored only in the folders you choose (your workspace) and in your user settings on your own computer. The app does not upload them anywhere.

## Network access

The app works fully offline. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **Check for Updates** (installer versions only, not the Microsoft Store version) — the app asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release of Designer Report and, if you choose to update, downloads the installer from GitHub. No personal information is sent; GitHub receives the usual technical request information (such as your IP address), as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Links you click** — *Documentation*, *Report an Issue* and *Donate* open pages in your web browser (GitHub, Buy Me a Coffee). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Data sources you configure yourself (for example a JSON file) are read from the location you specify.

## Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about the installation as described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The app itself does not receive that information.

## Donations

Donations are handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee. The app never sees your payment details.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/mechya/Designer-Report-Release/issues>.
